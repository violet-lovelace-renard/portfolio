About me

Hey there! You can call me Violet, although I like to go by many names depending on my mood. I'm a computer programmer, music listener, video game player, and purple enjoyer. 💜

My names

You could say I'm a median system. In other words, I have different "parts" of me that embody different traits, but they're not distinct enough to be separate personalities. Each of them has a name that they'd like to be called — if you see me wearing a name tag, that's what it's for.
